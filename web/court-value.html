Why a court, rather than one judge?

Give both sides a voice. Check the evidence. Know when to stop.

The comparison, including its trade-off

Same 65 synthetic cases and model (deepseek:deepseek-chat). Both configurations include policy checks. The full court also adds advocate briefs and rebuttals; prompts and workflow differ. This comparison does not isolate debate as the sole cause.

Exact core result is not the same as safe handling: a correct escalation can match the reference without resolving the dispute. “Eligible to act” is a simulation flag, not an actual payment.

Mean stored latency: single judge 8.6s; court 26.5s. Better handling costs more time and calls. We have not measured production savings or ROI.

Both advocates read F9 the same way and the stipulation binds: the ground for F3 is general_route_consent [F9][RD-5].
