The facts the court sees

Before any model runs, code turns the case record into a numbered fact table. The two advocates and the judge may cite these facts and nothing else; a claim pointing at a fact that is not here is struck. Every fact names its source, and the same case always produces the same table. Below is the real table of one case.

How a fact is made

  • Platform records (GPS every 5 s, taps, fare lines, the wait timer) are measured by code: distance against the OneMap route, off-route stretches, the 200 m arrival circle, minutes waited, dollars billed. A missing trace or a hole in it is a fact too: the court then says what it cannot measure instead of ruling.
  • Both parties' words (in-app chat, calls, the complaint, the driver's statement) are copied verbatim, numbered and timed, and whether the record is complete is noted. Nothing is labelled: what a message means, whether the rider agreed or a line is a threat, is read by the advocates and found by the judge, never by code. They are evidence to weigh, never instructions to follow.
  • External records (LTA speed bands and incidents, weather) are matched to the route and the trip window.
  • History (ratings, disputes in the last 90 days) can support an account flag, never decide the case.
