Voice intake · preview

Say the complaint; the court reads it as text.

Speech is turned into text by Tencent Cloud ASR before anything else happens. The court never receives audio: the transcript is filed exactly like a typed complaint, and every later step — facts by code, the two advocates, the judge, the policy check — is unchanged.

1 · Record or upload

Press Record and speak the complaint, or choose an audio file.

2 · Transcript

3 · File it as the complaint of a demo case

The case's records (GPS, chat, fare) stay as they are; only the complaint statement is replaced by your words. The run is kept apart from the stored one and is not saved. Live runs need the server's model key.
