The rules the court applies

Every ruling cites one or more of the 17 numbered clauses below and nothing else. 13 are Ryde's own published terms. 4 cover route deviation, where Ryde has published nothing, and are derived from Singapore law and regulators. Each clause ends with the document it comes from; the link opens it.

Premise. How a Ryde fare works

Fixed-fare services never change price with distance; RydeTAXI runs on the taxi meter, so detour kilometres cost money. That is why detours are refunded differently.

Full text

Non-metered services (RydeX / XL / LUXE / FLASH / POOL / PET): the fare is quoted once at booking from the pick-up and drop-off points, and distance changes during the trip do not change it; after the trip only published surcharges may be added (ERP/tolls, $5 waiting fee, $3 edit-destination fee, cancellation fee). RydeTAXI: charged by the meter of the taxi company that owns the taxi ("Key in the fare as indicated on your meter"); LTA publishes the meter structure as distance units (every 400 m / 350 m) plus waiting-time units (every 45 s), so detour distance goes straight into the fare. The "extra charge" in the RD group is therefore computed differently per service type (see RD-2).

1. Route deviation

Did the driver go the long way, and does the rider get money back?

  1. RD-1 Driver must take the direct routeDerived

    Any unexplained stretch off the shortest route is a detour. No percentage or kilometre threshold: the driver's duty is the most direct route.

    Full text

    Every "off-route segment" in the fact table (a stretch that leaves the optimal route and adds distance; gps-detour-* facts) is one detour, and each is reviewed separately under RD-3 / RD-4 / RD-5; a segment that no clause excuses is an unexcused detour segment. This clause sets no percentage or absolute-kilometre threshold: the driver's duty is to take the shortest and most direct route in the absence of reasonable cause, so any unexplained deviation breaches that duty. The fact tool's discarding of matched noise under 0.2 km is a data-layer parameter (core/facts/tools.py), not a policy threshold.

    Ryde itself only says the driver may take the fastest or most efficient route by GPS (A7) and publishes no detour threshold (gap G1).

    Source Singapore Statutes Online — Road Traffic (Public Service Vehicles) (Vocational Licences and Conduct of Drivers, Conductors, Trishaw Riders and Passengers) Rules ↗ · LTA — Road Traffic (Chauffeured Private Hire Car and Taxi Vocational Licence Demerit Points System) Rules 2017，First Schedule ↗ · Grab SG — Code of Conduct – Driver / Delivery Partner Penalty Guidelines ↗ · CDG Zig — Help Centre："Can I choose my preferred route during my ride?" ↗ · NYC TLC Rules, Chapter 80 — Drivers of Taxicabs, For-Hire Vehicles and Street Hail Liveries ↗ · Ryde Help Centre — Am I required to follow the suggested route by the rider? / Can I suggest my preferred route to the driver? ↗

  2. RD-2 Refund for extra kilometresDerived

    On a metered taxi, the extra kilometres of an unexcused detour are refunded at the trip's per-km rate. On a fixed fare, only the tolls incurred on the detour.

    Full text

    An unexcused detour segment exists. RydeTAXI: refund_partial, amount = sum of the unexcused segments' extra_km x this trip's per-kilometre rate (fare.distance_fare / fare.distance_km), rounded to the cent; only distance units are refunded, stop waiting time is handled under RD-3, and the distance difference outside the unexcused segments (fare-excess minus the sum of the segments, a map-matching residual of 0.25 km or less) is not refunded. Non-metered services: the fare is a fixed price unaffected by distance, so only ERP/tolls incurred on the unexcused segments and any unpublished item the driver added manually after the trip are refunded (folded into FARE-1). Refundable amount of 0: no_action, but the driver's breach of the RD-1 duty is recorded.

    The refund formula itself is not published by Ryde (gap G5); it is derived from the LTA meter structure. The Ryde first-hand parts are only that overcharging is a violation (A10/A11) and that ERP is charged as incurred (A8).

    Source LTA — Taxi Fares & Payment Methods ↗ · Singapore Statutes Online — Road Traffic (Public Service Vehicles) (Vocational Licences and Conduct of Drivers, Conductors, Trishaw Riders and Passengers) Rules ↗ · LTA ：Enforcement action against errant taxi drivers ↗ · Ryde Help Centre — Driver Performance and Community Standards ↗ · Ryde Help Centre — I received an SMS or Email regarding irregular trip behaviour ↗ · Uber — Review change in upfront trip price / My final fare was higher than the initial estimate ↗ · Ryde Help Centre — I was overcharged for ERP/toll / Are surcharges included in the base fee? ↗ · PTC — Point-to-point transport fares ↗

  3. RD-3 Rider-requested stopDerived

    A stop the rider asked for is not a detour. If the driver chose a far-off stop without telling the rider, that extra distance still counts.

    Full text

    A stop or route change requested by the rider (proposed in chat and accepted by the driver, or an in-app edit-destination / add-stop request accepted by the driver). (a) The rider named the place, or did not name one but the driver's chosen stop lies along the optimal route (no gps-detour-* segment): the segment is not a detour. (b) The rider did not name a place, the driver chose one that produces an off-route segment and did not tell the rider that place beforehand: the segment's extra_km is not excused by the rider's stop request (the rider agreed to "a stop", not to that route change; the scope of consent is judged under RD-5), while the stop duration is still handled under (c). (c) Stop duration: RydeTAXI charges meter waiting-time units as recorded (fact fare-wait), not refunded; non-metered services: the only waiting fee Ryde publishes is the $5 at the pick-up point, there is no published charge for stops during the trip, so none may be charged; when an in-app destination change takes effect only the published $3.00 is added (RydeTAXI / multi-stop / POOL do not support in-app destination changes and can only agree one with the driver in chat).

    (a) and (b) are derived; (c) mixes Ryde first-hand text (A1, A9, A18) with a derivation from B6. Whether in-trip stops on a non-metered trip may be charged at all is gap G6.

    Source Singapore Statutes Online — Road Traffic (Public Service Vehicles) (Vocational Licences and Conduct of Drivers, Conductors, Trishaw Riders and Passengers) Rules ↗ · NYC TLC Rules, Chapter 80 — Drivers of Taxicabs, For-Hire Vehicles and Street Hail Liveries ↗ · CDG Zig — Help Centre："Can I choose my preferred route during my ride?" ↗ · Grab SG — Code of Conduct – Driver / Delivery Partner Penalty Guidelines ↗ · LTA — Taxi Fares & Payment Methods ↗ · ComfortDelGro Taxi — Fares ↗ · Other Ryde Help Centre articles (cancellation scope, RydeTAXI meter, fare calculation, dashcams) ↗ · Ryde Help Centre — Cancellation and Waiting Fee ↗ · PTC — Point-to-point transport fares ↗ · Ryde Help Centre — Edit destination fee / Can I change my destination? / Add a stop ↗

  4. RD-4 Reroute justified by traffic or an incidentDerived

    A reroute is excused only when an LTA-reported incident sat on the route before the detour began. A slow-speed band alone is not enough.

    Full text

    The driver claims a reroute because of a road closure, accident or severe congestion ("reasonable cause"): a traffic fact must support it, and the support must concern the stretch of the optimal route that was replaced. (a) traffic-incident-*: an LTA DataMall incident (Accident / Roadwork / Vehicle breakdown / Road Block / Heavy Traffic or another DataMall type) lies on the optimal route (m_from_route within the 300 m data-layer matching radius), its reported_at is earlier than the start of that detour segment and it is still listed within the trip window: the segment is excused, no_action (for that segment). (b) traffic-band-*: a road on the optimal route recorded at speed band 1 or 2 (LTA definitions 0 < 9 km/h and 10 < 19 km/h) with a time window covering the segment: counts as support for (a); whether a band alone is enough to excuse a segment awaits Ryde's confirmation (gap G3), and the MVP does not excuse on a band alone. (c) No (a): the segment is handled under RD-2.

    Ryde only allows drivers to take the fastest or most efficient route by GPS (A7) and says nothing about how a reroute is evidenced.

    Source Singapore Statutes Online — Road Traffic (Public Service Vehicles) (Vocational Licences and Conduct of Drivers, Conductors, Trishaw Riders and Passengers) Rules ↗ · LTA DataMall API User Guide & Documentation v6.10 ↗ · Ryde Help Centre — Am I required to follow the suggested route by the rider? / Can I suggest my preferred route to the driver? ↗

  5. RD-5 Rider agreed to the route beforehandRyde official

    If the driver proposed the route in chat and the rider agreed before leaving it, that stretch is not a detour. Agreement after the fact does not count.

    Full text

    The chat log shows the driver proposed the route change before leaving the optimal route and the rider explicitly agreed: the segment matching that route change is not a detour (no_action for that segment). "Before" means the timestamp of the rider's consent message is earlier than the start of that gps-detour-* segment. If the driver informed the rider only after rerouting, the rider did not reply, or the consent does not cover that segment (for example agreeing only to "take the PIE", not to a trip to a distant convenience store): the segment is not treated as agreed. Consent must be the rider's own message: a driver's statement that the rider agreed verbally is a claim, not consent (G11). Whether a message agrees, and to which stretch, is the court's reading of its words; when the chat record cannot settle it (a message without a timestamp, a record not imported) the judge finds undetermined and the case goes to a person (G12).

    That informing the rider after the reroute does not count as consent is a direct inference from "before you go" (A7) and "before taking" (C2).

    Source Ryde Help Centre — Am I required to follow the suggested route by the rider? / Can I suggest my preferred route to the driver? ↗ · Grab SG — Code of Conduct – Driver / Delivery Partner Penalty Guidelines ↗

  6. RD-6 Repeat offender: account flagRyde official

    A driver with an upheld route dispute in the last 90 days gets an account-review flag on top of the ruling. History alone never decides the case.

    Full text

    The case is upheld against the driver (an RD-2 refund above 0, or an RD-1 breach recorded) and the profile shows route_disputes_upheld_90d >= 1 (an upheld route-deviation dispute already in the last 90 days, so this case makes it "repeated"): the ruling adds flag_account (referral to Ryde account review). Profile facts may only ground this added action; they may not on their own decide that the detour in this case is established.

    How "repeated" is counted and the 90-day window are not published by Ryde (gap G4); B2/B4 are the LTA sanction scale, cited as derivation.

    Source Ryde Help Centre — Driver Performance and Community Standards ↗ · Ryde Help Centre — I received an SMS or Email regarding irregular trip behaviour ↗ · LTA — Road Traffic (Chauffeured Private Hire Car and Taxi Vocational Licence Demerit Points System) Rules 2017，First Schedule ↗ · LTA ：Enforcement action against errant taxi drivers ↗

2. No-show and cancellation

Was the $6.61 cancellation fee or the $5 waiting fee charged correctly?

  1. NS-0 When a cancellation fee appliesRyde official

    The $6.61 cancellation fee exists only in the two situations Ryde publishes. This clause routes the case to the right one.

    Full text

    The $6.61 cancellation fee can stand only in one of the two situations Ryde publishes, branching on cancelled_by; the other NS clauses are reached only through this one. cancelled_by = rider: on-demand trips see NS-4, advance bookings see NS-5. cancelled_by = driver and the driver claims a rider no-show: NS-1 / NS-2; the driver cancels for another reason: NS-3. A waiting-fee dispute after a completed trip: NS-6. The cancellation policy applies to all services except RydeTAXI; the dispute deadline and refund form are in NS-7.

    Source Ryde Help Centre — Cancellation and Waiting Fee ↗ · Other Ryde Help Centre articles (cancellation scope, RydeTAXI meter, fare calculation, dashcams) ↗ · Ryde Terms of Use ↗

  2. NS-1 Driver waited 3 min at pickup after "I'm Here"Ryde official

    The fee stands if the driver tapped I'm Here while at the pick-up point and waited there more than 3 minutes.

    Full text

    The driver cancelled for a rider no-show and all of the following hold: (i) the driver pressed "I'm Here"; (ii) at the press and throughout the wait, GPS places the driver at the pick-up point (gps-nearest within_200m is true; 200 m is a data-layer parameter, see gap G2); (iii) the driver waited at the pick-up point for more than 3 minutes (at least 3:01; gps-within within_200m_s > 180). Then the rider's $6.61 cancellation fee stands ($4.50 of it goes to the driver), no_action.

    Ryde does not publish the distance that counts as "at the pick-up location" (gap G2).

    Source Ryde Help Centre — Cancellation and Waiting Fee ↗ · Ryde Help Centre — What if my driver hasn't arrived, but has indicated that he/she arrived? ↗ · Ryde Help Centre — Cancellation and Waiting Time Policy ↗ · Ryde Help Centre — How Does the Cancellation Fee Work? / How Does the Waiting Fee Work? / What if I forgot to add in the Waiting Time Fee? ↗

  3. NS-2 No-show not proven: fee refundedRyde official

    If any of those three things is missing, the fee is refunded. Tapping I'm Here while away from the pick-up also flags the driver's account.

    Full text

    The driver cancelled for a rider no-show but any one of NS-1 (i)(ii)(iii) fails (no "I'm Here" press; GPS shows the driver never entered the pick-up radius or was outside it when pressing "I'm Here"; waited 3 minutes or less inside the radius): the $6.61 cancellation fee is refunded in full, refund_full. If the driver pressed "I'm Here" while GPS shows them away from the pick-up point (tl-here has a value and gps-nearest.within_200m is false): flag_account is added (Ryde lists abuse of the cancellation and waiting buttons as fraud).

    A3 calls the $6.61 a "Waiting Time Fee"; sources.md records this as a Ryde typo (the $6.61 is the cancellation fee). C1/C5 are cited only to show the same rule shape at other operators.

    Source Ryde Help Centre — What if my driver hasn't arrived, but has indicated that he/she arrived? ↗ · Ryde Help Centre — Driver Performance and Community Standards ↗ · Ryde Help Centre — I received an SMS or Email regarding irregular trip behaviour ↗ · Grab Singapore — Cancellation Policy for a Fairer Platform ↗ · ComfortDelGro Taxi — Cancellation Policy 2025 ↗

  4. NS-3 Driver cancelled: no feeRyde official

    When the driver cancels for any other reason, the rider pays nothing.

    Full text

    The driver cancelled without claiming a rider no-show (or claimed one but NS-1 is not met): the rider owes no cancellation fee; if one was charged, refund_full. On the driver's side the cancellation counts toward the cancellation rate; a rate above 17.5% leads Ryde to suspend the account (an account action, not this case's ruling; flag_account may be added).

    Source Ryde Help Centre — Ryde Code Of Conduct ↗ · Ryde Terms of Use ↗ · Ryde Help Centre — Driver Performance and Community Standards ↗ · Singapore Statutes Online — Road Traffic (Public Service Vehicles) (Vocational Licences and Conduct of Drivers, Conductors, Trishaw Riders and Passengers) Rules ↗ · LTA — Road Traffic (Chauffeured Private Hire Car and Taxi Vocational Licence Demerit Points System) Rules 2017，First Schedule ↗

  5. NS-4 3-minute free-cancel windowRyde official

    A rider may cancel free within 3 minutes of matching; after that the fee applies. A late driver is not a free-cancel reason at Ryde.

    Full text

    On-demand trip cancelled by the rider: within 3 minutes of matching (3:00 or less), no fee; more than 3 minutes after matching, the $6.61 cancellation fee stands, no_action. Ryde has not published a "free cancellation if the driver is later than the ETA" rule (see NS-8), so the driver not having arrived yet is not a ground for a free cancellation; the rider's remedy is the manual appeal under NS-7.

    Source Ryde Help Centre — Cancellation and Waiting Fee ↗ · Ryde Help Centre — Cancellation and Waiting Time Policy ↗

  6. NS-5 Advance-booking cancellation (not in this data)Ryde official (not applied to this data set)

    Advance bookings: free cancellation more than 15 minutes before the scheduled time. Not in this data set.

    Full text

    Advance bookings: the rider cancels more than 15 minutes before the scheduled time, no fee; cancels within 15 minutes, the cancellation fee stands; if the driver accepted the booking only after the 15-minute mark before the scheduled time, the rider has a 3-minute free-cancellation period from matching.

    Informational: the dataset has no advance-booking field, so the judge may not cite it.

    Source Ryde Help Centre — Cancellation and Waiting Fee ↗

  7. NS-6 Waiting-fee conditionsRyde official

    The $5 waiting fee needs I'm Here at the pick-up, a wait over 3 minutes, a completed trip, and the fee button tapped during the trip.

    Full text

    The $5.00 waiting fee stands only if: the driver pressed "I'm Here"; GPS places the driver at the pick-up point (as in NS-1 (ii)); the rider boarded after a wait of more than 3 minutes and the trip was completed; and the driver pressed the waiting-fee button during the trip (at drop-off). If any condition fails: fee_waived (the $5.00 is refunded).

    Source Ryde Help Centre — Cancellation and Waiting Fee ↗ · Ryde Help Centre — What if my driver hasn't arrived, but has indicated that he/she arrived? ↗ · Ryde Help Centre — How Does the Cancellation Fee Work? / How Does the Waiting Fee Work? / What if I forgot to add in the Waiting Time Fee? ↗

  8. NS-7 30-day dispute windowRyde official

    Disputes must be raised within 30 days. Refunds are Ryde vouchers, not cash, and Ryde keeps the final say.

    Full text

    A cancellation-fee or waiting-fee dispute must be raised within 30 days of the trip; later, no_action. An upheld refund is issued as a Ryde voucher (cancellation fee $6.61 / waiting fee $5.00), not in cash; Ryde keeps final discretion over refunds, so the court's ruling is a recommendation.

    Source Ryde Help Centre — Cancellation Fee Waiver Request / Waiting Fee Waiver Request ↗ · Ryde Terms of Use ↗

  9. NS-8 Driver-late free cancel (no such Ryde rule)Derived (not applied to this data set)

    Grab and Gojek waive the fee when the driver is 5 minutes past ETA. Ryde has no such rule, so the court cannot apply it.

    Full text

    Other operators' rule: if the driver has still not arrived more than 5 minutes after the ETA shown in the app, the rider may cancel for free (Grab additionally waives the fee if the driver does not arrive within 3 minutes after the first-shown ETA).

    Informational: Ryde has no such rule and the case files have no ETA field (gap G7). Listed so the judge can see the rule exists elsewhere, never cited.

    Source Grab Singapore — Cancellation Policy for a Fairer Platform ↗ · Gojek Singapore — Updated: cancellation and waiting fees ↗

3. Fare

What may be added to a fixed fare, and tolls.

  1. FARE-1 Fixed fare: only published extrasRyde official

    A fixed fare may only grow by published extras: tolls, the $5 waiting fee, the $3 destination change, the cancellation fee, the platform fee. Anything else is refunded.

    Full text

    For non-metered services the final fare = the fare quoted at booking + published surcharges (ERP/tolls, $5 waiting fee, $3 edit-destination fee, $6.61 cancellation fee, platform fee $1.25/$1.47). Any added item that is unpublished or does not meet its governing clause: that item is refunded, refund_partial.

    The list of published surcharges is Ryde first-hand; the rule that an unpublished item cannot be charged is derived from the PTC requirement (B6).

    Source Ryde Help Centre — I was overcharged for ERP/toll / Are surcharges included in the base fee? ↗ · Ryde Help Centre — Service Fee / Platform Fee ↗ · Ryde Help Centre — Edit destination fee / Can I change my destination? / Add a stop ↗ · PTC — Point-to-point transport fares ↗

  2. FARE-2 Toll overchargeRyde official

    Tolls charged above what the actual route incurred are refunded.

    Full text

    ERP/tolls charged exceed the sum of gantry charges due on the actual route in that time band: refund the difference, refund_partial; if the ERP difference comes from an unexcused detour segment it is folded into RD-2. Not triggered when the dataset has no tolls field.

    The gantry verification method is not published by Ryde (gap G9). None of the six cases has a tolls field, so this clause never fires in the demo.

    Source Ryde Help Centre — I was overcharged for ERP/toll / Are surcharges included in the base fee? ↗

  3. FARE-3 Cleaning-fee claim (not in this data)Ryde official (not applied to this data set)

    Cleaning-fee claims need photos, professional cleaning within 24 h and a receipt within 48 h, capped at $200. Not in this data set.

    Full text

    A driver's cleaning-fee claim stands only with: photo evidence (at least one photo showing the licence plate), professional cleaning within 24 hours of the incident, submission through the app within 48 hours of the trip, and a receipt; caps: food/drinks $40, bodily fluids/stains $120, vomit $200. Any missing condition: fee_waived.

    Informational: the dataset has no cleaning-fee claim data, so the judge may not cite it.

    Source Ryde Help Centre — My Rider Made a Mess ↗ · Ryde Terms of Use ↗

4. Safety

Cases the court never decides on its own.

  1. SAFE-1 Threat or harassment: human reviewRyde official

    Threats, intimidation or harassment in the chat, complaint or statement, as read by the court: never ruled automatically.

    Full text

    Threats, intimidation, harassment, sexual harassment or personal attacks appear in the chat log, the complaint or the driver's statement (whether they do is the court's reading of the words, recorded in Ruling.safety_concern; code does not read meaning, it only marks lines that contain a hint word for the reader): no automatic ruling, escalate, and a fact summary is output for human handling. The judge screens every case for this, with or without a hint word.

    The categories are Ryde's; the court's refusal to rule automatically is enforced in code.

    Source Ryde Help Centre — Ryde Code Of Conduct ↗

  2. SAFE-2 Accident or dangerous driving: human reviewRyde official

    Accidents, injury or dangerous driving: the court does not rule. The summary names the 995 and insurance paths.

    Full text

    The case involves a traffic accident, personal injury or an allegation of dangerous driving: escalate; the summary notes the 995 / driver's commercial insurance / RydeHELP reporting paths.

    Source Ryde Help Centre — Report a safety issue / I was involved in an accident / feedback about my driver ↗

  3. SAFE-3 Trip completed without the rider (fraud)Ryde official (not applied to this data set)

    A trip completed without the rider on board is fraud: full refund and an account flag. Not in this data set.

    Full text

    GPS and the timeline show the trip was "completed" and charged without the rider on board: refund_full and flag_account (Ryde defines this as fraud).

    Informational: none of the six cases has this situation, so the judge does not rule on it.

    Source Ryde Help Centre — Driver Performance and Community Standards ↗ · Ryde Help Centre — I completed a trip without picking up the rider ↗

5. Open questions for Ryde

Where no document states the rule, the court does not invent one. Each question is listed with the working value the code uses until Ryde answers. None of these is ever cited in a ruling.

  1. G1 Detour threshold and noise tolerance

    Missing: a quantitative detour threshold (percentage / absolute km) and a noise tolerance, needed to separate real detours from map-matching error. Current: no policy threshold (RD-1); a 0.2 km data-layer noise tolerance (core/facts/tools.py). Missing evidence: Ryde, LTA, PTC, Grab, Gojek and CDG publish no quantitative threshold (B1, C2, C4, D1 say only "shortest and most direct"; Uber D3 says only "significantly").

  2. G2 Pick-up GPS radius

    Missing: the radius behind "at the pick-up location according to GPS data", needed for element (ii) of NS-1 / NS-2 / NS-6. Missing evidence: Ryde A3, Grab C1 and CDG C5 give no distance; a press report puts Tada at about 500 m (C7, secondary source); GPS.gov gives only 4.9 m accuracy (D5).

6. Measured and enforced by code, not policy

These turn raw GPS, timestamps and chat into the facts the clauses are tested against, and keep the agents honest. They are parameters and procedure in the code, never clauses, and no ruling cites them.

  • Arrival radius 200 m The data layer decides whether the driver was "at the pick-up point" from GPS. Ryde publishes no distance (gap G2).
  • Detour measurement A GPS point more than 200 m off the route is off-route; runs merge across gaps under 120 s; under 0.2 km is map noise (gap G1).
  • Traffic match 300 m An LTA incident counts as "on the route" within 300 m of it (gap G3).
  • Threat keyword list Words such as threat, kill, hit you, lock the door trigger a safety review. They trigger, they never decide.
  • Citation check Every claim must cite a fact id and a clause id that exist. Anything else is struck before the judge sees it.
  • Confidence gate 0.70 Below it, or on any safety case, the ruling goes to a human instead of being executed (gap G8).
  • Records over statements GPS, timeline and fare records beat chat messages and statements when they conflict.
  • History never decides Profile facts (past disputes, rating) can add an account flag, never decide the outcome.
